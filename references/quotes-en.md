## 《道德经》9 句

### Q1. 反者道之动；弱者道之用
**章节**：第四十章
**英文定型**：`"Reversal is the movement of the Dao; weakness is the method of the Dao." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963, Penguin Classics)`
**备用（文学版）**：`"To revert from the full is to move in the Dao; to be depleted is to be replenished." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1934)`
**适用**：决策反转点、最坏情况预判、势的临界点

---

### Q2. 知人者智，自知者明；胜人者有力，自胜者强
**章节**：第三十三章
**英文定型**：`"Knowing others is intelligence; knowing oneself is enlightenment. Conquering others is strength; conquering oneself is might." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)`
**备用（通俗版）**：`"Knowing others is to be wise; knowing yourself is enlightened. Mastering others requires force; mastering yourself requires strength." (trans. Addiss & Lombardo, 1993)`
**适用**：决策前的自我反思、四线同根整合

---

### Q3. 知止而后有定，定而后能静，静而后能安，安而后能虑，虑而后能得
**章节**：《大学》（非《道德经》——R6.5 张冠李戴高发区）
**英文定型**：`"Having stopped, you are firm. Having been firm, you can be still. Having been still, you can be at peace. At peace you can think. Thinking, you attain." (trans. Ezra Pound, 1951)`
**备用（学术版）**：`"Having attained this purport, one is afterwards fixed in it. Being thus fixed, one becomes calm..." (trans. James Legge, 1893)`
**适用**：决策收束、势能起点

---

### Q4. 少则得，多则惑
**章节**：第二十二章
**英文定型**：`"The few are those from whom one gains; the many are those from whom one suffers." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)`
**备用（文学版）**：`"It is by way of Many that one goes astray; by way of Few that one arrives at the Dao." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1934)`
**适用**：警惕"既要又要"、最大陷阱警告

---

### Q5. 物壮则老，是谓不道，不道早已
**章节**：第三十章
**英文定型**：`"If a thing has grown strong, it grows old. This is contrary to the Dao; that which is contrary to the Dao soon ends." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)`
**备用（文学版）**：`"When a thing has reached its full strength, it begins to wane..." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1934)`
**适用**：盛极必衰警告、最高点判断

---

### Q6. 上善若水
**章节**：第八章
**英文定型**：`"The highest good is like water." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)`
**适用**：柔性力量、攻心为上

---

### Q7. 知足不辱，知止不殆，可以长久
**章节**：第四十四章
**英文定型**：`"One who knows that enough is enough will always have enough." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)`
**适用**：边界设定、节制欲望

---

### Q20. 上善若水，水善利万物而不争
**章节**：第八章（Q6 全句版）
**英文定型**：`"The highest good is like water. Water benefits the ten thousand things and does not contend with them." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)`
**备用（通俗版）**：`"The highest good is like water. Water gives life to the ten thousand things and does not struggle with them." (trans. Addiss & Lombardo, 1993)`
**适用**：攻心为上、不争之争、顺势而为。**Q6 是 Q20 的极简版**，短场景用 Q6，深度展开用 Q20。

---

### Q21. 大方无隅，大器晚成，大音希声，大象无形
**章节**：第四十一章
**英文定型**：`"The great square has no corners; the great vessel is late in completion; the great music is faintly heard; the great image has no form." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)`
**适用**：反对过早自我定义、大器晚成的反潮流、长期主义。"无隅" = 没有边界 = 真正伟大的形态不被框定。

## 《论语》6 句

### Q9. 君子不器
**章节**：《论语·为政》
**英文定型**：`"The gentleman is not an implement." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)`
**适用**：拒绝工具化、伦理校准

---

### Q10. 父母在，不远游，游必有方
**章节**：《论语·里仁》
**英文定型**：`"While his parents are alive, a son may not go abroad. If he does go abroad, he must have a fixed destination." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)`
**备用（更准确版）**：`"While his parents are alive, the son should not travel far. If he must travel, he should let his parents know where he is going." (trans. James Legge, 1893)`
**适用**：出差/家庭决策（ACPTE-TOD 试跑 1 命中的金句）

---

### Q11. 中庸之为德也，其至矣哉
**章节**：《论语·雍也》
**英文定型**：`"The virtue of the Mean is something extreme." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)`
**适用**：避免极端、儒家平衡

---

### Q12. 不义而富且贵，于我如浮云
**章节**：《论语·述而》
**英文定型**：`"Wealth and rank acquired through unrighteousness are to me like floating clouds." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)`
**适用**：义利之辨、伦理边界

---

### Q13. 己所不欲，勿施于人
**章节**：《论语·颜渊》
**英文定型**：`"Never do to others what you would not like them to do to you." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)`
**适用**：忠恕之道、人心向背

---

### Q22. 学而时习之，不亦说乎
**章节**：《论语·学而》
**英文定型**：`"To learn and at due times to repeat what one has learnt, is that not after all a pleasure?" (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)`
**备用**：`"Is it not pleasant to learn with a constant perseverance and application?" (trans. James Legge, 1893)`
**适用**：长期主义、重复的力量、学习节奏。**常被错误地译为'learning is happy'——实际是关于'时习'（适时重复）的节奏智慧**，不是简单的快乐学习。

## 《孙子兵法》4 句

### Q8. 兵者，诡道也
**章节**：《孙子兵法·计篇》
**英文定型**：`"War is deception." (trans. Thomas Cleary, 1988)`
**备用**：`"All warfare is based on deception." (trans. Lionel Giles, 1910)`
**适用**：博弈论、信息差

---

### Q14. 知己知彼，百战不殆
**章节**：《孙子兵法·谋攻篇》
**英文定型**：`"Know the enemy and know yourself; in a hundred battles, you will never be in danger." (trans. Thomas Cleary, 1988)`
**备用**：`"If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles." (trans. Lionel Giles, 1910)`
**适用**：信息差盘点、博弈分析

---

### Q15. 上兵伐谋，其次伐交，其次伐兵，其下攻城
**章节**：《孙子兵法·谋攻篇》
**英文定型**：`"The highest form of warfare is to attack the enemy's plans; the next best is to attack his alliances; the next is to attack his army; the worst is to besiege his cities." (trans. Lionel Giles, 1910)`
**备用**：`"The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting." (trans. Thomas Cleary, 1988)`
**适用**：路径设计、不战而胜

---

### Q16. 先为不可胜，以待敌之可胜
**章节**：《孙子兵法·军形篇》
**英文定型**：`"Position yourself so that you cannot be defeated, and wait for the enemy's moment of vulnerability." (trans. Thomas Cleary, 1988)`
**备用**：`"Invincibility lies in the defence; the possibility of victory in the attack." (trans. Lionel Giles, 1910)`
**适用**：保守路径、不可替代性积累

---

## 《周易》3 句

### Q17. 时止则止，时行则行，动静不失其时
**章节**：《易·艮卦·彖传》
**英文定型**：`"When the time has come to stop, then stop. When the time has come to move, then move. Only by not overstepping the times can one act without mishap." (trans. Richard Wilhelm, 1924; English trans. Cary F. Baynes, 1950)`
**适用**：易家时位、时机判断

---

### Q18. 亢龙有悔
**章节**：《易·乾卦·上九》
**英文定型**：`"The dragon overreaches and has cause for remorse." (trans. Richard Wilhelm, 1924)`
**备用**：`"The flying dragon is at the height." (trans. Edward Shaughnessy, 1996)`
**适用**：盛极必衰警告

---

### Q19. 履霜，坚冰至
**章节**：《易·坤卦·初六》
**英文定型**：`"When you tread on frost, you know that the cold of winter is near." (trans. Richard Wilhelm, 1924)`
**备用**：`"Treading on frost, the solid ice approaches." (trans. Edward Shaughnessy, 1996)`
**适用**：萌芽阶段判断、势的早期信号
