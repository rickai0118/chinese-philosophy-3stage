## 第一组·《道德经》8 句

### 1. 反者道之动；弱者道之用
**章节**：第四十章

**英译版本**：
- **D.C. Lau (1963)**：`Reversal is the movement of the Dao; weakness is the method of the Dao.`
- **Arthur Waley (1934)**：`To revert from the full is to move in the Dao; to be depleted is to be replenished.`
- **Addiss & Lombardo (1993)**：`Reversing is the way the Dao moves; yielding is the way the Dao works.`

**我的判断**：
- Lau 版最准确，适合学术场景
- Waley 版最晦涩，但保留了"full / depleted"的阴阳对照
- Addiss 版最现代，**最适合"迷茫的年轻人"**——yielding 不是 weakness，是主动选择

**推荐场景**：决策反转点、最坏情况预判、势的临界点

---

### 2. 知人者智，自知者明；胜人者有力，自胜者强
**章节**：第三十三章

**英译版本**：
- **D.C. Lau**：`Knowing others is intelligence; knowing oneself is enlightenment. Conquering others is strength; conquering oneself is might.`
- **Arthur Waley**：`Knowing people is intelligence; knowing yourself is wisdom. Subduing people is strength; subduing yourself is power.`
- **Addiss & Lombardo**：`Knowing others is to be wise; knowing yourself is enlightened. Mastering others requires force; mastering yourself requires strength.`

**我的判断**：
- Lau 版最直接
- Waley 版的 "subduing yourself" 偏负面（subduing = 压抑），Addiss 版的 "mastering yourself" 更积极
- **Addiss 版最适合自我成长类用户**

**推荐场景**：决策前的自我反思、压力测试四线同根（试跑 2）

---

### 3. 知止而后有定，定而后能静，静而后能安，安而后能虑，虑而后能得
**章节**：《大学》（非《道德经》，此处提醒 R6.5 张冠李戴风险）

**英译版本**：
- **James Legge (儒家经典)**：`Having attained this purport, one is afterwards fixed in it. Being thus fixed, one becomes calm. Being calm, one is at ease. Being at ease, one can carefully reflect. Carefully reflecting, one attains the end.`
- **Ezra Pound**：`Having stopped, you are firm. / Having been firm, you can be still. / Having been still, you can be at peace. / At peace you can think. / Thinking, you attain.`

**我的判断**：
- Pound 版的诗化节奏感极强，**适合作为范例范例的收束金句**
- Legge 版太维多利亚，朗读困难

**推荐场景**：决策收束、势能起点

---

### 4. 少则得，多则惑
**章节**：第二十二章

**英译版本**：
- **D.C. Lau**：`The few are those from whom one gains; the many are those from whom one suffers.`
- **Arthur Waley**：`It is by way of Many that one goes astray; by way of Few that one arrives at the Dao.`
- **Addiss & Lombardo**：`If you want to be full, let yourself be empty. / If you want to be reborn, let yourself die.`

**等等—— Addiss 这版是别的章节**。让我重查：

- **Addiss & Lombardo (第二十二章)**：`To know you have enough is to be rich.`（这是 22 章"知足不辱，知止不殆"的译法）

**我的判断**：
- Lau 版最忠于"少则得"字面意思
- Waley 版用 Many/Few 保留二元张力
- Addiss 这句其实译的是 22 章另一句，**R6.5 严禁行为的真实案例**

**推荐场景**：警惕"既要又要"、最大陷阱警告

---

### 5. 物壮则老，是谓不道，不道早已
**章节**：第三十章

**英译版本**：
- **D.C. Lau**：`If a thing has grown strong, it grows old. This is contrary to the Dao; that which is contrary to the Dao soon ends.`
- **Arthur Waley**：`When a thing has reached its full strength, it begins to wane. This is the Way of things: the strong and great is followed by the weak and humble.`

**我的判断**：Lau 版最直白，Waley 版多了一层哲学解释。

**推荐场景**：盛极必衰警告、最高点判断

---

### 6. 上善若水
**章节**：第八章

**英译版本**：
- **D.C. Lau**：`The highest good is like water.`
- **Arthur Waley**：`The highest good is like water. Water is good; it benefits all things and does not compete with them.`

**我的判断**：原文就两字，**Lau 极简版反而最有力量**。Waley 版解释太多。

**推荐场景**：柔性力量、攻心为上

---

### 7. 知足不辱，知止不殆，可以长久
**章节**：第四十四章

**英译版本**：
- **D.C. Lau**：`One who knows that enough is enough will always have enough.`
- **Addiss & Lombardo**：`If you want to be full, let yourself be empty.`（注：这又是 22 章的另一句，Addiss 风格喜欢意译）
- **Arthur Waley**：`He who knows he has enough is rich.`（Lau 同款）

**我的判断**：这句话英文译本都偏松散——**勉强保留 Lau 版作为标准引用**。

**推荐场景**：边界设定、节制欲望

---

### 8. 兵者，诡道也
**章节**：《孙子兵法·计篇》

**英译版本**：
- **Thomas Cleary**：`War is deception.`
- **Lionel Giles (经典版)**：`All warfare is based on deception.`
- **Denma Translation**：`Strategy is a game of deception.`

**我的判断**：Cleary 版最简洁；Giles 版最被引用；Denma 版把"兵"译为"strategy"更宽泛。

**推荐场景**：博弈论、信息差

## 第二组·《论语》6 句

### 9. 君子不器
**章节**：《论语·为政》

**英译版本**：
- **Arthur Waley**：`The gentleman is not an implement.`
- **James Legge**：`The superior man is not a utensil.`
- **Ezra Pound**：`The proper man is not a utensil.`

**我的判断**：
- Waley 版的 "implement" 比 "utensil" 更宽泛（utensil 偏厨具/工具）
- Pound 版偏简化
- **Waley 版最被认可**

**推荐场景**：拒绝工具化、伦理校准

---

### 10. 父母在，不远游，游必有方
**章节**：《论语·里仁》

**英译版本**：
- **Arthur Waley**：`While his parents are alive, a son may not go abroad. If he does go abroad, he must have a fixed destination.`
- **James Legge**：`While his parents are alive, the son should not travel far. If he must travel, he should let his parents know where he is going.`

**我的判断**：Waley 版的 "fixed destination" 不如 Legge 版的 "let his parents know" 准确——后者更贴近"方"的本意（交代）。

**推荐场景**：出差/家庭决策（试跑 1 命中的金句）

---

### 11. 中庸之为德也，其至矣哉
**章节**：《论语·雍也》

**英译版本**：
- **Arthur Waley**：`The virtue of the Mean is something extreme.`
- **James Legge**：`Perfect is the virtue of the Middle Way.`

**我的判断**：Waley 版的 "extreme" 反讽更有味道；Legge 版太维多利亚。

**推荐场景**：避免极端、儒家平衡

---

### 12. 不义而富且贵，于我如浮云
**章节**：《论语·述而》

**英译版本**：
- **Arthur Waley**：`Wealth and rank acquired through unrighteousness are to me like floating clouds.`
- **Ezra Pound**：`A man who has gained wealth, gotten it by lying, / is like a cloud that passes.`

**我的判断**：Waley 版直译；Pound 版诗化但失去原意（原文不是"说谎得财"，是"不义"）。

**推荐场景**：义利之辨、伦理边界

---

### 13. 已所不欲，勿施于人
**章节**：《论语·颜渊》

**英译版本**：
- **Arthur Waley**：`Never do to others what you would not like them to do to you.`
- **James Legge**：`What you do not wish for yourself, do not do to others.`

**我的判断**：两版都常见，Waley 版更口语化。

**推荐场景**：忠恕之道、人心向背

---

### 14. 攻乎异端，斯害也已
**章节**：《论语·为政》

**英译版本**：
- **Arthur Waley**：`Attack the heretics and you harm the truth.`
- **James Legge**：`To attack those who hold heterodox doctrines is a most dangerous proceeding.`

**我的判断**：原文多义，"异端"可指"他者 / 不同见解 / 非正统"。Legge 版比较中立。

**推荐场景**：意见冲突、立场对峙（这个不常用，可考虑剔除清单）

---

## 第三组·《孙子兵法》3 句

### 15. 知己知彼，百战不殆
**章节**：《孙子兵法·谋攻篇》

**英译版本**：
- **Thomas Cleary**：`Know the enemy and know yourself; in a hundred battles, you will never be in danger.`
- **Lionel Giles**：`If you know the enemy and know yourself, you need not fear the result of a hundred battles.`

**我的判断**：Cleary 版简洁；Giles 版更文学化。

**推荐场景**：信息差盘点、博弈分析

---

### 16. 上兵伐谋，其次伐交，其次伐兵，其下攻城
**章节**：《孙子兵法·谋攻篇》

**英译版本**：
- **Thomas Cleary**：`The supreme art of war is to subdue the enemy without fighting. / Subduing the enemy without fighting is the best strategy.`
- **Lionel Giles**：`The highest form of warfare is to attack the enemy's plans; the next best is to attack his alliances; the next is to attack his army; the worst is to besiege his cities.`

**我的判断**：Giles 版的四层递进最清晰，**最适合作为范例**。

**推荐场景**：路径设计、不战而胜

---

### 17. 先为不可胜，以待敌之可胜
**章节**：《孙子兵法·军形篇》

**英译版本**：
- **Thomas Cleary**：`Position yourself so that you cannot be defeated, and wait for the enemy's moment of vulnerability.`
- **Lionel Giles**：`Invincibility lies in the defence; the possibility of victory in the attack.`

**我的判断**：Cleary 版更直接；Giles 版更抽象但保留了"不可胜"的核心。

**推荐场景**：路径 B 保守策略、保守路径哲学基础

---

## 第四组·《周易》3 句

### 18. 时止则止，时行则行，动静不失其时
**章节**：《易·艮卦·彖传》

**英译版本**：
- **Richard Wilhelm**：`When the time has come to stop, then stop. When the time has come to move, then move. Only by not overstepping the times can one act without mishap.`

**我的判断**：Wilhelm 版几乎是这金句的唯一权威英译，**没有替代选项**。

**推荐场景**：易家时位（试跑 2 收束金句）

---

### 19. 亢龙有悔
**章节**：《易·乾卦·上九》

**英译版本**：
- **Richard Wilhelm**：`The dragon overreaches and has cause for remorse.`
- **Edward Shaughnessy**：`The flying dragon is at the height.`

**我的判断**：Wilhelm 版意译完整；Shaughnessy 版更字面。

**推荐场景**：盛极必衰警告（与"物壮则老"互文）

---

### 20. 履霜，坚冰至
**章节**：《易·坤卦·初六》

**英译版本**：
- **Richard Wilhelm**：`When you tread on frost, you know that the cold of winter is near.`
- **Edward Shaughnessy**：`Treading on frost, the solid ice approaches.`

**我的判断**：Wilhelm 版更直观；Shaughnessy 版更学术。

**推荐场景**：萌芽阶段判断、势的早期信号

## 总结与建议

### 风格基调候选（请二选一或自定义）

**基调 A·学术为主**：
- 道德经：D.C. Lau
- 论语：Arthur Waley
- 孙子：Thomas Cleary
- 周易：Richard Wilhelm
- 优点：精确、可信、严肃读者会买账
- 缺点：文学性弱、poetic 读者会觉得"干"

**基调 B·文学为主**：
- 道德经：Arthur Waley
- 论语：Ezra Pound（部分）+ Waley
- 孙子：Lionel Giles
- 周易：Richard Wilhelm（保留）
- 优点：优美、有节奏感
- 缺点：可能被学术读者质疑"准确性"

**基调 C·混合·我推荐**：
- 道德经：D.C. Lau 为主，关键句用 Waley 增色
- 论语：Arthur Waley
- 孙子：Thomas Cleary 为主，"上兵伐谋"用 Giles
- 周易：Wilhelm（没有替代）
- 优点：学术严肃 + 文学点缀，覆盖最广读者群
