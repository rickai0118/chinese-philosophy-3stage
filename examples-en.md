## Example 1 · Life Decision (Mid-Difficulty)

**User Question**: "I'm considering taking a higher-paying job that requires frequent travel. I'm worried about my family time."

*(ACPTE-TOD triggers "ethical subject" → 儒家前置 → 顺序 Conf → Dao → Mil)*

---

### Stage 3 · Confucian Calibration (前出)

> "While his parents are alive, a son may not go abroad. If he does go abroad, he must have a fixed destination." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)

**白话 interpretation**: Confucius never said "never travel." He said: if you do travel, you must give your family a *destination* — a stable expectation, a clear account of where you'll be and when you'll return.

This hits the core of your dilemma: you're not asking "should I travel?" — you're asking "do I have the capacity to make my family feel stable *even when I'm not there*?"

**Three Confucian questions**:

1. **Yi (义)**: Who is this travel for?
   - For the family's future material security → Yi, with the precondition that they consent
   - To prove "I can earn more" → Li (利) disguised as Yi, the most dangerous form

2. **Zhong-Shu (忠恕)**: Empathy check —
   - If your spouse/parents rarely see you, how do *they* interpret this "high-paying" job?
   - "He's working hard for us" and "He's absent so we can be rich" are two completely different family emotions.

3. **Zhong-Yong (中庸)**: Is there a third "middle way"?
   - Not "fully travel" or "fully stay" — but a *designed rhythm*: max X days/month away, absolute presence the rest of the time.
   - True 中庸 is not compromise — it's an **active design** that lets everyone rest easy.

**人心向背**:
- Accept but family is uneasy → even the money you bring home feels like "guilt compensation." Children remember your absence, not your income.
- Decline but your heart is bitter → the "presence" at home carries the weight of "I sacrificed for you." Children remember the heavy air, not your company.
- **The real design problem is not "accept or decline," but "how to keep family emotions from collapsing after you accept."**

---

### Stage 1 · Daoist Reading of the Trend

> "Reversal is the movement of the Dao; weakness is the method of the Dao." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)

**白话 interpretation**: Stability and travel-pressure appear to be opposites, but they are two sides of the same 势 (trend). Stability at its peak is already transitioning to plateau — stability *is* the ceiling. Travel pressure at its peak is the germination phase — the pressure hides the biggest reversal.

**Life-cycle prediction**:
- Whatever you choose, in 3 years you'll look back at this decision.
- The real inflection point is not "which one," but **"do you know what you actually want"** — if you choose stability to escape pressure, stability becomes a new pressure. If you choose travel to escape the ceiling, the ceiling returns in another form.

**Warning — "too full" trap**:
The biggest hidden danger is *wanting both*. If you currently feel "I want both the money and the family time," that is a sign of 福兮祸所伏 (fortune's shadow).

---

### Stage 2 · Military Reading of the Game

> "Position yourself so that you cannot be defeated, and wait for the enemy's moment of vulnerability." (trans. Thomas Cleary, 1988)

**白话 interpretation**: Before you attack the opportunity, make sure you cannot be destroyed.

**Information asymmetry**:
- What you see: salary gap, travel frequency, family needs.
- What you don't know (critical): is this "high-paying" opportunity non-substitutable? Could you find a similar-paying role with more controllable rhythm elsewhere?
- The real game is not just you vs. the company — your family is also a player, not a backdrop.

**Path A · Aggressive (正)**:
Take the travel job. Treat the high-pressure as a training ground.
- Risk: misjudging the industry carries huge sunk cost; emotional capital depletes first.

**Path B · Conservative (奇)**:
Stay in the current job, but **prototype the new idea on the side**. Use the low-intensity hours of your current role for high-density exploration — build an MVP over 6-12 months.
- Risk: if the MVP fails, you may miss promotion windows in your current job. But you gain **"knowing yourself"** — the military strategist's most valued asset.

**Path C · Military's Best Strategy**:
**Negotiate "hybrid mode"** before committing.
- Don't get trapped in the binary "accept or decline." Go back and negotiate: can the travel be front-loaded (first 6 months intense, then remote)? Can it be project-based instead of full-time? Can you join as a partner rather than an employee?
- 兵家术语: "上兵伐谋" — make them redesign the game, not play within their rules.
- Risk: they may say "no." But if they do, **it reveals that what they're buying is not your ability but your time and obedience** — and that company isn't worth joining.

---

### Closing (易家 Optional)

> "When the time has come to stop, then stop. When the time has come to move, then move. Only by not overstepping the times can one act without mishap." (trans. Richard Wilhelm, 1924; English trans. Cary F. Baynes, 1950)

**白话 interpretation**: You are at 艮 (Mountain) — facing the mountain. The best move is to **stop and see clearly** before deciding whether to climb over.

- If your heart already echoes (you hear the call), that's "时行" — Path A or C is more timely.
- If your heart is still uncertain, the time hasn't come — try Path B first, and when the time comes, you'll naturally know.

---

### Summary

> "Having stopped, you are firm. Having been firm, you can be still. Having been still, you can be at peace. At peace you can think. Thinking, you attain." (trans. Ezra Pound, 1951)

You are not at the moment of deciding — you are at the moment of **first knowing where to stop**. When the "stop" is clear, the decision reveals itself.

You don't need "you can do it." You need 知止.

## Example 2 · Power-Struggle Story Design (Brief)

**User Request**: "I'm writing a power-struggle reversal. The protagonist is a double agent."

*(ACPTE-TOD triggers "story design" → 兵家先行 → Dao → Conf)*

---

### Stage 1 · Military Reading — Calculating Forces

> "Know the enemy and know yourself; in a hundred battles, you will never be in danger." (trans. Thomas Cleary, 1988)

**Three power blocs**:
- **A (公开主公)**: Holds military power but unaware of the inner traitor.
- **B (暗中反贼)**: Holds intelligence network but underestimates A's counter-surveillance.
- **Protagonist (double agent)**: Holds A's trust + B's core intel simultaneously.

**Information asymmetry**: A doesn't know protagonist is B's. B doesn't know protagonist has already confided in A.
**Key resource**: Protagonist's **credibility** — the core currency of all games.

---

### Stage 2 · Daoist Reading — Hidden State

> "The highest good is like water." (trans. D.C. Lau, 1963)

**白话 interpretation**: Water wins by yielding. The protagonist's "double-agent identity" is the *缺* (deficiency) — but this *缺* protects him:
- A trusts him because B's occasional small losses give him a credible record of setbacks.
- B trusts him because he never *fully* wins — always leaving a thread for B to see.

**Reversal point**: The moment the protagonist fully wins or fully loses, the *缺* breaks — both sides will suspect.

**Prediction**: The climax MUST occur when the protagonist is forced to actively reveal himself once — that's the breaking of *缺* and the pole of dramatic tension.

---

### Stage 3 · Confucian Reading — Inner Conflict

> "The gentleman is not an implement." (trans. Arthur Waley, 1938)

**白话 interpretation**: A 器 (utensil) serves one function. A 君子 (gentleman) refuses to be reduced to one role.

In the final moment, the protagonist must choose between two *义 (righteousness)*:
- Loyalty to A (the promise)
- Loyalty to B (the ideal he once identified with)

**人心向背**: This is where the drama lives — the author must NOT hand the protagonist a "right answer." Both 义 stand. Let the audience see that even the "villain" had a moral claim.

---

### Closing (易家)

> "When you tread on frost, you know that the cold of winter is near." (trans. Richard Wilhelm, 1924)

**白话 interpretation**: The earliest signals of reversal are quiet. The protagonist's first small "loss" (which A notices and B is satisfied with) is the frost — winter (full revelation) is approaching.
